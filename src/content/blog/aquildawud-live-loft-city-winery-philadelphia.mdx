PHILLY!!! I am still thinking about Friday night at The Loft at City Winery Philadelphia. The room upstairs was lit in purple and blue, candles and wine glasses on the tables, and a band set up under the square white LOFT sign on that wall of stacked wood. RNB After Dark brought us AQUILDAWUD LIVE, and that young man gave us a damn SHOW. I'm Harry Hayman, and Friday night is still on my mind. This one sent me home talking. Two screens on either side of the stage carried the RNB After Dark flyer with AQUILDAWUD LIVE printed on it.

## A Friday Night Upstairs at City Winery

The listing called it [AQUILDAWUD LIVE, presented by City Winery Philadelphia and RNB After Dark](https://tickets.citywinery.com/event/aquildawud-live-fawydz), Friday, November 13, at 7:30 in The Loft. It promised special guests and some surprises, and that is about as honest as event copy gets. My photographs caught more than one voice at the microphone over the course of the night, with keys on both sides of the stage, a drummer in the back, and a bass player holding down the middle.

If you have not been up there, The Loft is the smaller of the two rooms at City Winery. When the venue opened at 10th and Filbert in the Fashion District in 2019, [The Philadelphia Inquirer described the upstairs Loft as a 150 capacity room](https://www.inquirer.com/entertainment/music/city-winery-philadelphia-line-up-fashion-district-los-lobos-20190801.html) where everybody sits, with a 350 seat main room downstairs. That seated, close setup matters for a singer. In a room like that, nobody has to fight a standing bar crowd to be heard. The tables come right up to the edge of the stage.

## Whose Son Is This?

Congrats to my friends Fatin and Aja of Kindred the Family Soul on a job well done. Think about it. I have spent years enjoying the music those two have given us, and there I was on a Friday night in Philly, watching their son headline a room and make it his own.

For anybody who does not know the family, Kindred the Family Soul is the husband and wife duo of Fatin Dantzler and Aja Graydon, and they came out of the Philadelphia neo soul movement. According to [the Kindred the Family Soul entry on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kindred_the_Family_Soul), Jill Scott introduced them to Hidden Beach Recordings while they were performing weekly at the Black Lily nights in Philly back in 2000, and their debut album, Surrender to Love, came out in February 2003. More than twenty years on, they are still performing together.

Their family grew right alongside the music. Back in April 2021, [the Philadelphia Sunday Sun](https://www.philasun.com/local/kindred-the-family-soul-wants-you-to-pull-up-online-to-celebrate-their-new-album-auntie-unc-at-their-virtual-backyard-bbq-concert-april-3/) wrote that the couple's family had grown from one child to six, and that their oldest son Aquil was a college student and also a recording artist. That oldest son is the man whose name was on the screens Friday night.

## Who Aquildawud Is

Aquildawud is the stage name of Aquil Dantzler. [His Sofar Sounds artist profile](https://www.sofarsounds.com/artists/aquildawud) describes him as a soul and R&B artist from Philadelphia, the eldest son of Kindred the Family Soul, who credits his parents with his passion for music. The same profile traces his drive back to high school, when he started collaborating with a rapper named Tanner Hicks, performing at open mics, recording original songs and making music videos with their friends. It lists his first two singles, For the Lovers and Summer Day, both from 2019, and a debut project called Born To Be Loved. It also names the artists who inspire him: Donny Hathaway, Marvin Gaye, Musiq Soulchild, Lauryn Hill and Erykah Badu.

He has been playing well beyond Philadelphia, too. That Sofar profile lists his performances for Sofar audiences in Washington, New York, Philadelphia and Miami.

The City Winery listing for Friday put his arrival plainly: "Born into RnB Music royalty, he has music flowing through his DNA." That line first ran in [a 2024 Philly Soul Now feature by Chike Evans called Aquildawud Has Next](https://phillysoulnow.com/2024/06/24/aquildawud-has-next/), which also points out that he has worked to set himself apart artistically from his famous parents. Friday night he made the room his own. The family name is one I love. Friday night, the name I walked out talking about was AQUILDAWUD.

One of my photographs is of a black shirt with his illustrated face on it, AQUILDAWUD across the chest in purple and blue, and Born To Be Loved written underneath. That title has been with him for a while now, and it was good to see it printed on a shirt at the show.

## The Room Showed Up

And ARNETTA JOHNSON was in the house! I looked around and thought, Damn, Philly really does show up for its own.

If you do not know Arnetta, you should. [Jazz Philadelphia's Hometown Hero profile](https://jazzphiladelphia.org/hometown-hero-arnetta-johnson/) tells the story of a trumpeter who picked up the horn as an eighth grader in Camden, came through Creative Arts High School, started crossing the bridge to Philly for the jam sessions at Chris' Jazz Cafe, and went on to arena stages alongside Beyoncé. [The Inquirer wrote about her in 2019](https://www.inquirer.com/entertainment/music/25-this-camden-trumpeter-has-toured-with-beyonce-twice-now-shes-bringing-her-act-philly-20190718.html) when she was 25 and had already toured with Beyoncé twice. She leads her own band, SUNNY, and plays what she calls disruptive jazz. When a musician of that caliber spends her Friday night in the audience for somebody else, that tells you something about the room.

That is the part I keep coming back to. A young artist headlining is one thing. A young artist headlining with established Philly musicians in the audience is how a scene keeps itself going. That is the kind of support that turns one good night into a career.

## The People Who Got Us in the Door

Big love to Toya Haynes and Philly Soul Now for getting the word out, putting tickets in people's hands through the giveaway, and giving Philly artists the attention they deserve. [Philly Soul Now](https://phillysoulnow.com/), where Toya is the editor, keeps a concert calendar, runs ticket giveaways, reviews shows and builds a New Music playlist on Spotify for the local soul scene. They had [RNB After Dark Presents Aquildawud at City Winery](https://phillysoulnow.com/event/rnb-after-dark-presents-aquildawud-at-city-winery/) on that calendar, and they were featuring his music on [their New Music playlist back in September 2023](https://phillysoulnow.com/2023/09/26/new-music-aquildawud-dj-royale-suzann-christine-and-more/). That is what paying attention early looks like.

And MEDINA, thank you for helping produce the night! Anybody who has ever put on a show knows how much work happens before the first person walks through the door. Somebody books the room. Somebody builds the flyer that ends up on two screens. Somebody makes sure the band has what it needs and the guests know where to go. I see you, and I appreciate you.

RNB After Dark deserves its credit as well. Give people a good room, a real band and a singer worth hearing, and they will come.

## Go See Him Before the Rooms Get Bigger

And Aquil, brother, TAKE A BOW!!!

The good news is that you do not have to wait long for another chance. [City Winery's listing for Kindred The Family Soul NYE](https://tickets.citywinery.com/event/kindred-the-family-soul-nye-6czeuv) has Fatin and Aja on the Main Stage on December 31, with two shows at 7:00 and 10:30, billed with Aquildawud. The Friday listing also said he was coming off an opening set for Jill Scott at the Met, which makes for a nice bit of Philadelphia symmetry, since Jill Scott was the one who introduced his parents to the label that gave them their first record deal.

Philly, go see this man live. Bring somebody with you. I want to see what happens when he gets an even bigger room. Follow him on [his own Instagram page, @aquildawud](https://www.instagram.com/aquildawud/), so you know when he is playing next, and tell a friend who has not heard him yet.
